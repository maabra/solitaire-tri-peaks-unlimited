# Two connected activities

Treasure Trail unlocks at campaign level 2,500. Each subsequent new campaign win
earns one roll. A roll consumes one token and moves Pip 1–6 spaces around a
24-space trail. Crossing each sixth space awards a sticker pack. Completing a
lap awards extra coins, a two-star pack and a one-use Wild. Other landings also
award coins. Rewards are saved immediately.

Matching Garden unlocks at level 12,000. Every two new campaign wins earns one
seed. One seed pays for a pair of tile flips. Matching pairs award 20 coins;
completing six pairs awards 150 more coins, a two-star pack and a one-use Joker.
Mismatches remain visible briefly before turning back. Revealed and matched
tiles, seeds and progress survive reloads. New rounds shuffle deterministically.

Existing levels, endless replays and repeat reward claims do not generate these
currencies. Packs join the existing album, helpers join the existing inventory,
and coins use the ordinary wallet. Activities have direct return-to-cards links,
map journey shortcuts and Pip guides. No online services or purchases are used.
