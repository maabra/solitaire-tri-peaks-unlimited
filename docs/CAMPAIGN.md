# Illustrated campaign ? 2.5.0

The campaign is 100 chapters ? 100 levels = 10,000 levels. The 100 original chapter mural, card-back and table sets determine the cap. Chapter 101 and later previously repeated those sets; those destinations are removed from Travel, Card Studio and the overworld. Endless remains separate.

CampaignContent.cs is the canonical C# catalog for chapter titles, total levels, mechanic anchors and late activity unlocks. The content exporter projects it into generated-content.mjs for the common browser/Windows renderer. Early first-chapter activity introductions stay spaced as before. Treasure Trail opens at 600, Matching Garden at 2,500 and Shuffle at 125. Mechanic introductions span gift cards at 50 through color seals at 9,600, with difficulty pressure reaching its bounded ceiling at 10,000. Long gardens reach all four authored sizes within the campaign.

Saves are validated against the previous 480,000-level envelope before migration. Retained level records keep their IDs; progress is not compressed into artificial completions. The visible campaign stops at 10,000. Retired stars, scores and collections are archived in LegacyCampaign; repeated earned cosmetic IDs map to their original chapter art. Wallets, hearts, items and active boards are preserved. An active retired campaign hand changes to endless mode, preserving its board. Migration is idempotent, and malformed negative or out-of-range legacy counters remain invalid.

Verification: all 10,000 modern campaign winning witnesses and all 90 long gardens; 16 browser engine test files; 100 distinct chapter pictures loaded in a browser; actual final-level victory to endless; last Travel/Card Studio page bounds; legacy import/load/save round trips; 26 screens at three sizes; native full suite, a warning-free Release build, and the packaged Windows WebView smoke check (map, wheel, preview, legal win, stable card nodes, shop, Settings, Gem Garden and full save-bridge round trip). The README and existing documentation screenshots were recaptured from isolated sample profiles in the running game.
