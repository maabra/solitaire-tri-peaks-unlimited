MEADOW PEAKS - SOLITAIRE UNLIMITED 1.6

RUN
Double-click MeadowPeaks-1.6.exe (latest local build) or the release MeadowPeaks.exe.
Close an older game first to keep one process writing your save. No installer, internet or runtime download needed.
Windows 10/11 x64. The single EXE extracts its runtime cache automatically.
For the portable ZIP, extract the whole folder before launching its EXE.

PLAY
Clear an exposed card one rank above or below the pile. Suits do not matter.
Ace connects King and Two. Clear covering cards to expose the next cards.
Clear the whole board to win. Draw from stock for another rank.
There is NO level countdown. Take your time when no effect is active.
Hints show matches for 3 seconds, then need 15 seconds to recharge.
The initial match glow is brief. Hints and scout effects also expire.

ADVENTURE & REPLAY
480,000 generated levels, 24,000 chapters, 20 world palettes, 4-8 card rows.
The first four levels introduce the rules. Five-row boards arrive at 300, six at 700, seven at 1,300 and eight at 2,100.
Smaller boards stay in the rotation.
Every new campaign formation after the introduction is distinct, even after
normalizing horizontal translations and mirrors. All levels have tested paths.
Replay starts the same board for free. The dice button remixes fresh cards at
this level, also for free. Starting either replaces the current hand.
Scores and stars retain personal bests. Replays still pay smaller coin rewards.
Every 20 completed levels gives one chapter gift: 150 coins and one Wild.
Every 25th campaign level is a bonus level: +125 coins and one consumable on its
first completion. Replaying/remixing cannot farm its bonus reward.

PLAYTIME BUDGET
480,000 levels at an assumed one minute each = 8,000 campaign hours.
Up to 2,000 endless hours count toward the requested 10,000-hour content target.
This is an estimate, not a playtested duration guarantee or an endless lockout.
Real time depends on player speed, replays, helpers and time in Gem Garden.

COINS & CONSUMABLES
Start with 300 coins, two Wilds, two +5 helpers and one Remove helper.
Wild: 75 coins. Play any rank once within 12 seconds; unused Wilds expire.
+5 cards: 60 coins. Available when stock is empty.
Remove: 90 coins. Remove an exposed card, bypassing ice/lock/armor/vines.
Clover +1: 85 coins. Each rank clear adds one stock card for 10 seconds.
Fire: 70 coins. Ignore ice for 8 seconds; remaining ice returns afterward.
Shield: 80 coins. Bomb fuses stop advancing on moves for 10 seconds.
Scout: 35 coins. Show playable matches for 5 seconds.
Keypass: 90 coins. Ignore locked-card key requirements for 6 seconds.
Prune: 65 coins. Ignore vine streak requirements for 8 seconds.
Phase: 70 coins. Ignore sun/moon gate requirements for 6 seconds.
Peek: 40 coins. Show the next three stock cards for 6 seconds, in draw order.
These bypasses still require a legal rank; no boost lasts across hands.
Helpers share a 5-second recharge. Switch pages with the toolbar arrows.
Effects never carry into a new hand and there are no permanent power upgrades.
Menu, minimized-window and focus-loss time does not consume an effect.
Using a helper clears undo history. Failed actions do not consume inventory.
Three free undos per hand, then 15 coins each. Undo cannot rewind effect timers.
Effect durations, fuses and bonus stock are saved with the active hand.
Rank-created bonus stock is appended behind the existing stock.

SPECIAL CARDS & 100-LEVEL PROGRESSION
50: Gift cards. A rank clear appends three stock cards.
100: Ice. Clear its displayed number of other cards to thaw it.
200: Keys/locks. Collect the keys a lock requires; ranks still apply.
300: Bombs. Draws, clears and armor hits use a move of fuse, even while covered.
Clear before zero. Undo can rescue a burst, or replay free. No level countdown.
400: Up/down cards. Clear in that rank direction to append two stock cards.
King to Ace is up; Ace to King is down. Wild has no direction bonus.
500: Armor. Legal taps peel layers; the final legal tap clears by rank.
600: Lanterns. A clear shows playable matches for 4 seconds.
700: Clovers. A clear starts +1 stock per clear for 6 seconds.
800: Vines. Build the displayed consecutive clear streak before playing.
900: Shields. A clear pauses bomb fuses for 6 seconds.
2,000: Phase gates. The gate shows sun or moon; every successful clear, draw or
armor hit switches the board phase. The gate must match it. Invalid taps and
elapsed time do not flip phase. Undo restores phase. Phase helper bypasses it.
2,300: Color seals. Match both adjacent rank AND waste color: heart badge needs
red (hearts/diamonds), spade badge needs black (clubs/spades). Wild bypasses color.

Every 100 levels advances a tier. From 1,000, the emphasis rotates through
ice, locks, bombs, armor, vines and reserve-stock pressure. Early upgrades:
1,000 thicker ice; 1,100 two-key locks; 1,200 tighter bomb fuses;
1,300 three-hit armor; 1,400 three-clear vines; 1,500 fewer surplus draws.
Later cycles strengthen these within caps: three keys, four armor hits,
three-clear vines, two moves of fuse margin, ten core hazard cards (plus later gates/seals) and eight trimmed
reserve draws. Once capped, tiers keep rotating stronger combinations instead
of escalating a single mechanic without limit. Individual deals still vary.
The tier icon and number appear beside the formation name.
Only stock after the guaranteed-path draws may be trimmed.
Remove bypasses ice, locks, armor and vines, and can trigger key/lantern/clover/
shield effects. It does not give bonus stock. Ranks still apply to normal plays.

JOURNAL, FAVORITES & SHARED DEALS
The book on the home screen opens the journal. Daily picnic goals are optional:
win two hands (40 coins), clear 24 cards (50), earn 80 in Gem Garden (60).
Claim each completed goal once. Claiming all three adds one consumable.
Goals follow the local day, reset on a new day, and never impose a level timer.
The star beside a campaign level bookmarks it. Favorites appear in the journal,
with pages for larger collections. The star-arrow finds an earlier unfinished
three-star result. It opens the map without replacing your active hand.
The link beside Remix opens a share code. Copy is an explicit clipboard action.
The link on the home screen/journal imports a code. Opening it starts that deal
from the beginning after the usual hand-replacement confirmation.
Codes reproduce cards/formation and require this generator version (MP16).
Campaign codes cannot open locked levels. Old stored boards without current
metadata cannot be exported; finish them or remix to create a current deal.

BONUS OPENINGS
Every 25th campaign level retains its first-win coin/consumable reward.
From 600, bonus levels can start with Glow (8 seconds of match hints).
From 700, Harvest openings add +1 stock per clear for 6 seconds.
From 900, Shelter openings pause bomb fuses for 6 seconds.
The styles rotate. The clocks stop the effects, not the level. All these levels
also have a verified path with every timed effect expired before each action.

FREE COIN GARDEN
Swap adjacent gems to match 3+. Cascades pay immediately into the shared wallet.
Each round has 20 successful swaps. Invalid swaps cost no move. No entry fee,
level timer or life limit. Hints and dead-board recovery are free.

PRESENTATION & CONTROLS
Cards fly, armor shatters, vines bloom, ice cracks, matches sparkle, coins fly
and victories celebrate. Sound is optional. Reduced motion removes animations.
Tab/Enter: select/play. D: draw. U: undo. W: Wild. H: 3-second hint.
Esc: close/home. F11: maximize. Buttons work with mouse or keyboard.
Saves: %LOCALAPPDATA%\MeadowPeaks\progress.json (plus backup).
Old saves keep their original active board. New hands use the new generator.

FRESH CARD TIMERS (1.7)
Every stock draw recharges for 2 seconds. You may play cards during the wait.
Undo and saving do not bypass the recharge. Fresh-deal remix waits 30 seconds.
The +5 rescue helper creates five cards with a 10-second use window. Unused
rescue cards disappear; ordinary stock and card-gift rewards do not expire.
Effect clocks pause on menus, guides and inactive windows. No level countdown.
Browser: Settings can export/import compatible progress JSON files.

STORY FORMATIONS AND GEM GARDEN (1.8)
New hands use rotated fans, rings, ribbons, butterfly wings, tower groups and
other overlapping formations. Clear the cards covering a card to expose it.
Old saved boards and deal codes remain compatible.
Helpers appear as you discover their mechanics. Ice/lock/bomb/vine/phase helpers
are hidden from a level toolbar when that obstacle is absent or already cleared.
Fresh Garden recharges for ONE HOUR in real time. The board, moves and deadline
survive closing/reopening. Gem matches highlight before clearing and falling.
