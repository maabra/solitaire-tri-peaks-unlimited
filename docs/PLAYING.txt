MEADOW PEAKS — SOLITAIRE UNLIMITED

Single EXE: double-click MeadowPeaks.exe. No installation or other files needed.
Its bundled runtime extracts to a local cache automatically on first launch.

Extract the entire ZIP to a folder, then double-click MeadowPeaks.exe.
Windows 10/11, x64. No installation, internet, account, or runtime download needed.
Keep the executable together with its bundled files and language folders.

ADVENTURE
Continue adventure opens a map of 2,000 levels. Pick the golden next level.
You can replay any completed level. Previous/Next and Go to level browse the map.
The levels are reproducible seeded deals with three different board layouts.

Play any uncovered card one rank above or below the waste card. Suits do not
matter. Ace wraps around to King. Clear covering cards to expose new cards.
Clear the entire board to win. Draw from the stock when you need another rank.
There is no timer. Every level has a legal solution without buying helpers.

ENDLESS MEADOW
An ongoing sequence of new seeded hands. Each completed hand grows your garden
and pays coins. No final level, lives, energy, or entry fee.

COINS & HELPERS
Start with 300 coins, two Wilds, two +5 helpers, and one Remove helper.
Wild (75 coins): any exposed card can be played on it; your streak continues.
More cards (60 coins): adds five cards, usable only when your stock is empty.
Remove (90 coins): choose any exposed card to remove. Click again to cancel.
Helpers are inventory items: buy in the shop, then use from the bottom toolbar.
Using a helper clears undo history so it cannot be reclaimed by undoing.
Three free undos per hand; further undos cost 15 coins each.
First level win: 100 + 10 per star + 2 per stock card left.
Replay win: 30 + 10 per star + 2 per stock card left.
Endless win: 100 + 10 per star + 2 per stock card left.
Stars: one for clearing, two for a best streak of 5+, three for 9+.
Collect a 75-coin daily gift on the home screen. Every five cleared hands earns
an 80-coin garden gift, also collected on the home screen.

GEM GARDEN
Always free, including at zero coins. Swap two neighboring gems to make a line
of three or more. Matches disappear, gems fall, and cascades can follow.
Each removed gem earns 2 coins times its cascade depth. Coins enter your wallet
immediately. Invalid swaps cost no moves. Play 20 successful swaps per round,
then start another free round. The free hint identifies a legal swap.
Open it through the home screen, the coin shop, or the empty-stock prompt.
Your solitaire hand waits while you play. Return using Back to my hand.

KEYBOARD
Tab / Shift+Tab: move focus. Enter / Space: activate the focused button.
D: draw. U: undo. W: Wild. H: hint. Escape: close dialog / return home.
F11: maximize or restore the window. Settings include sound, card glow,
and reduced motion. Sound is off by default and is never required.

SAVING
Your wallet, inventory, level scores/stars, garden, settings and current
solitaire hand save automatically to:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\MeadowPeaks\progress.json
A .bak file holds the previous valid save. Undo history and an unfinished
Gem Garden round are session-only; earned coins save immediately.
Starting a different solitaire hand asks before replacing your current hand.
Replay restarts the same deal; spent coins and helpers stay spent.
